Makeup Education

3 Day Intensive Makeup Education

Professional training over three consecutive days, working on models. Build the skills, confidence and artistry to work behind the chair.

Book your place

Throughout your training, you will master:

  • The art of professional colour matching for every skin tone and undertone.
  • Curating a makeup kit with carefully selected product recommendations for both quality and versatility.
  • Skin preparation and creating the perfect canvas for long-lasting makeup.
  • Signature soft glam techniques, seamless blending, and flawless complexion application.
  • Client consultations, face analysis, and tailoring each makeup application to enhance individual features.
  • Professional hygiene and industry best practices.
  • Techniques to improve efficiency, confidence, and overall artistry behind the chair.

To support your learning beyond the course, you'll receive an exclusive RS Artistry Makeup Manual filled with professional tips, techniques, and product guidance to reference throughout your career.

Upon successful completion, you will also receive an RS Artistry Certificate of Completion, recognising your dedication and achievement throughout the training.

More than just learning makeup, this course is about building confidence, refining your artistry, and developing the skills to create an exceptional experience for every client.
