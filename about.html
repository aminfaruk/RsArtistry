Meet the artist

The Artist

Silvia

Founder & Lead Artist

Hi, I'm Silvia, the founder of RS Artistry and a Sydney-based Makeup Artist with over 9 years of experience in the beauty industry.

Since launching RS Artistry in 2018, my passion has been helping women feel confident, beautiful, and celebrated through makeup. Specialising in soft glam and bridal makeup, I believe in enhancing your natural features rather than covering them.

My journey began after noticing a lack of inclusivity within the industry, particularly for women of colour. I wanted to create a space where every client feels seen, understood, and confident that their unique beauty will be enhanced with care and expertise.

Today, I'm proud to work with clients of all skin tones and backgrounds, providing a personalised experience tailored to each individual. Whether it's your wedding day, a special event, or simply a moment to invest in yourself, my goal is for you to leave my chair feeling like the most confident version of you.

I look forward to welcoming you into my chair and creating a luxurious experience that leaves you feeling your most confident self.

Our story

Beauty found in the quiet moments of transformation.

Founded on the belief that makeup is an extension of personal architecture, RS Artistry is more than a studio. It is a space where editorial technique meets individual character. We move away from the masks of traditional luxury to reveal the light already within every subject.

Our approach is defined by restraint. We prioritise skin health, tonal harmony and the precision of a single brushstroke. By focusing on the interplay of light and shadow, we create looks that stay timeless through every lens.
