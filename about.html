Our story

Beauty found in the quiet moments of transformation.

Founded on the belief that makeup is an extension of personal architecture, RS Artistry is more than a studio. It is a space where editorial technique meets individual character. We move away from the masks of traditional luxury to reveal the light already within every subject.

Our approach is defined by restraint. We prioritise skin health, tonal harmony and the precision of a single brushstroke. By focusing on the interplay of light and shadow, we create looks that stay timeless through every lens.

The Artist

Silvia

Founder & Lead Artist

Silvia specialises in the ethereal. Her signature style centres on luminous skin and tonal depth, so every client feels like an elevated version of their most authentic self.

With over a decade across editorial and bridal makeup, she brings a structured, architectural perspective to beauty, defined by clean lines and a deep understanding of facial geometry.

“Artistry is not about addition. It is about the careful selection of what remains.”
