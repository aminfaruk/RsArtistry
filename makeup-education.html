With over nine years of experience in the beauty industry, RS Artistry was founded with a clear purpose: to create a more inclusive and empowering makeup experience for every client.

Throughout my career, I noticed a significant gap in the industry. Beauty education, product recommendations, and makeup techniques often centred around lighter skin tones, leaving many women with deeper complexions underserved and underrepresented. This inspired me to build a business that prioritises diversity, education, and artistry across all skin tones.

For me, makeup is more than enhancing features. It is about confidence, self-expression, and feeling seen. Every face is unique, and every skin tone deserves the same level of expertise, understanding, and attention to detail. True artistry lies in knowing how to adapt techniques, colours, and products to complement the individual, rather than applying a one-size-fits-all approach.

This 1:1 class has been created to support your makeup journey with practical knowledge, professional insights, and techniques that can be tailored to you. Whether you're refining your everyday routine or building confidence in your skills, my goal is to help you understand not only how to apply makeup, but why certain products and techniques work best for different skin tones and features.

Thank you for choosing RS Artistry. I am honoured to be part of your makeup journey and look forward to helping you create looks that celebrate your individuality with confidence and ease.
